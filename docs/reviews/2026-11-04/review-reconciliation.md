## Implementation proposals refined rather than adopted verbatim

- **Firebase means behavior as well as imports/configuration.** FR-01's `stoolMerge` runs inside `sendOperation`'s existing `runTransaction` callback via `operationData`. Changing its missing-target behavior changes transaction semantics. FR-02's truly atomic merge and AG-04/CL-03's identity/location guarantees likewise require separate integration design and authorization. A fresh local scan can detect a known conflict but cannot close the interval before another client's commit. No report wording should promise atomic safety from a cached scan.
- **Do not simply catch-and-continue through a conflicted outbox.** Keep unresolved payloads recoverable and later independent entries represented, but retain ordering for dependent operations. An incomplete initial cache is not proof of an authoritative deletion. A recovery export must preserve unresolved intent, not merely the truncated projection. Any future catch/rebase/quarantine design needs those distinctions tested.
- **Durable ordering requires compatibility and writer coordination.** Seed from all three supported durable queue prefixes and compare numeric sequence values. Padding only newly written keys does not establish correct mixed-format order. A plain shared `localStorage` get/set counter is not an atomic allocator across tabs. The report proposes the requirement, not an untested implementation.
- **Erase can reuse an existing sender, but needs more than enqueueing deletes.** `{col,id,erase:true}` already reaches `deleteDoc`. Local `applyOperation` does not project day erasure, and durable targets/intent, progress, restart, concurrent new logging and Undo behavior need design. The pending-outbox guard does not prohibit every offline erase: an offline user with an empty outbox can enter the direct-delete loop. This corrects Antigravity's broader guard claim.
- **Refusals are not automatically nutrition consumed.** Gate confirmed consumption and label plans, but do not blanket-filter every bowl outcome or warning by `fedAt`. Preserve a recorded refusal until the product policy explicitly resolves its meaning.
- **Keep the n-day warning interval the same size.** The actual lower bound is `toDay(day) > toDay(today) - n`. Add `toDay(day) <= toDay(today)`; Antigravity's suggested `>=` lower bound would include an extra day.
- **Do not infer blocked iOS downloads from a timer.** The second CSV download is delayed, but neither reviewer tested this on a physical installed iPhone. That is still a Files/Share acceptance check, not a newly proven Safari defect. Likewise, browser rectangles do not prove native-keyboard or VoiceOver behavior.
- **Independent clients are the reproduction condition, not necessarily two physical devices.** Ordinary shared-origin tabs share local outbox storage and are not equivalent to the probes' independent stores. Do not generalize that fact into a proof that all same-device races are impossible. Empty-cache, notice-stack, erase and restart-clock cases do not inherently require another device.
